Privacy and booking information

This website helps you prepare a travel enquiry. Enquiry details stay in your browser until you choose WhatsApp or Gmail and send your message. This version has no customer accounts or online payments.

The destination map is drawn from bundled Natural Earth data and does not request your location or contact a map tile service. Opening Google Maps directions, Instagram, WhatsApp or Gmail connects you to those services, which have their own privacy policies. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process connection information.

Your journey

Vehicle availability, pickup arrangements, fare and payment method must be agreed directly with Tropical Breeze Taxis. Preparing an enquiry does not confirm a booking. Payment is arranged after drop-off. Discuss changes or cancellations directly before travel.
