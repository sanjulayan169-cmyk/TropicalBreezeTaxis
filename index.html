PERSONAL SERVICE, FROM START TO FINISH

How your journey works.

A quick conversation. A fare agreed together. A comfortable journey.

01

Tell us your plans

Contact us on WhatsApp, email, Instagram or phone. Share your vehicle choice, pickup location, date and time, and drop-off location.

02

Agree on your journey

We discuss availability, your itinerary and the fare with you. Your booking is confirmed only when we agree the details directly.

03

We come to you

We aim to arrive 15 minutes before your confirmed pickup time. We will keep you informed if traffic affects arrival.

04

Pay after drop-off

Enjoy the journey and pay the agreed fare after reaching your destination. Confirm the payment method with us when booking.
